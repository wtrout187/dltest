## 🎮 How to Use

### Main Screen (Home)
- See your **Level**, **XP**, and **Streak**
- Check your **Readiness Score** (aim for 85+!)
- View your **Progress** and **Weak Areas**

### Study Modes
1. **🧠 Study Mode** - Practice with varied questions (recommended to start!)
2. **⚡ Mock Test** - Take a full 25-question practice test
3. **🎯 Weak Areas** - Focus on topics you're struggling with
4. **🚦 Road Signs** - Master South Dakota road signs

### How Questions Work
1. Read the question carefully
2. Click your answer (A, B, C, or D)
3. Get instant feedback (correct/incorrect)
4. Read the explanation to learn
5. Click "Next Question" to continue

## 📊 Track Your Progress

### Readiness Score
Your readiness score shows how prepared you are:
- **0-30**: Just getting started
- **30-50**: Making progress
- **50-70**: Good progress, keep going!
- **70-85**: Almost ready!
- **85-100**: Ready to take the test! 🎉

### What Affects Your Score
- **Accuracy** (most important)
- **Number of questions practiced**
- **Daily streak**
- **Mastered topics**
- **Weak areas** (reduces score)
