A gamified, interactive study app designed to help Christian ace his South Dakota driver's license test! Built with love by Dad. ❤️

## 🌟 Live App

**Access the app here:** [https://wtrout187.github.io/dltest/](https://wtrout187.github.io/dltest/)

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Gamified Learning
- **XP System**: Earn 10 XP for every correct answer
- **Level Up**: Progress through levels as you learn
- **Daily Streaks**: Build momentum by studying every day
- **Achievements**: Unlock badges for milestones

### 🧠 Smart Learning Modes
- **Study Mode**: Practice with varied questions across all categories
- **Mock Test**: Take a full 25-question practice test (just like the real DMV test!)
- **Weak Areas**: Focus on topics you're struggling with
- **Road Signs**: Master South Dakota road signs and traffic signals

### 📊 Progress Tracking
- **Readiness Score**: See how prepared you are for the real test (aim for 85+!)
- **Category Progress**: Track performance in each topic area
- **Accuracy Tracking**: Monitor your overall and category-specific accuracy
- **Visual Progress**: Beautiful charts and progress bars

### 🎨 Modern Design
- **Dark/Light Themes**: Switch between themes for comfortable studying
- **Mobile Responsive**: Study on phone, tablet, or computer
- **Smooth Animations**: Engaging visual feedback
- **Accessibility**: Built with screen readers and keyboard navigation in mind

## 🎯 Readiness Score

Your readiness score is calculated based on:
- **Accuracy** (60 points): How many questions you answer correctly
- **Experience** (25 points): Total number of questions practiced
- **Streak** (15 points): Consecutive days of studying

**Aim for 85+ to be test-ready!**

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Buttons Not Working?
1. **Hard refresh** the page: Press `Ctrl+Shift+R` (Windows) or `Cmd+Shift+R` (Mac)
2. **Clear browser cache**: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data
3. **Try a different browser**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge

### Welcome Screen Stuck?
- Open browser console (F12) and type: `skipWelcome()`
- Or add `?skip=1` to the URL: `https://wtrout187.github.io/dltest/?skip=1`

### Progress Not Saving?
- Make sure cookies/local storage is enabled in your browser
- Don't use private/incognito mode
- Export your data regularly from Settings as a backup
